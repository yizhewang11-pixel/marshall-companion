设备与兼容性自检

Devices & compatibility

登录后自动在托盘运行。
Start in the system tray when you sign in.

不限制 Marshall 型号或代数，也可选择改名后的音箱。先在 Windows 配对，再选择系统识别到的蓝牙音频输出。各项功能需实际验证。若设备未列出，请确认 Windows 已建立蓝牙音频输出；部分驱动可能无法被识别。
No Marshall model or generation filter, including renamed speakers. Pair in Windows, then select a detected Bluetooth audio output. Features require testing. Missing devices must expose a Windows Bluetooth audio output; some drivers may not be recognized.

测试可能触发音箱连接提示音；不会主动断开已有连接，免打扰期间不会发送请求。
Testing may trigger the speaker connection sound. Existing connections are not disconnected; requests pause during quiet hours.

自动重连设置

Auto reconnect

断开后按此间隔尝试连接。连接成功即停止重试，夜间免打扰期间暂停。
Retry at this interval while disconnected. Stop retrying once connected; pause during quiet hours.

这是重连间隔；无声时播放保活音的等待时间仍为 8 分钟。
This controls reconnection retries. Keep-awake audio still waits for 8 minutes of silence.

夜间免打扰

Quiet hours

按本地时间每天重复，期间暂停重连和保活音。开始与恢复时间相同，表示全天免打扰。
Repeat daily in local time. Pause reconnection and keep-awake audio during this period. Identical start and end times mean all-day quiet hours.
